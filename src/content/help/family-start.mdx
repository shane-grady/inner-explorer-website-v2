Mindfulness grows when it shows up in more than one place. Practicing at home — even a few minutes before bed — extends what your child is learning at school. Here’s a gentle way to begin.

## Set up at home

If your child’s school uses Inner Explorer, you may have received a family invitation by email. Select **Accept invitation** and create a password to reach your family home screen. No invitation? Ask your child’s teacher whether family access is available at your school.

## Choose a device & space

Any phone, tablet, or computer works. Pick a quiet corner where you won’t be interrupted, turn the volume to a comfortable level, and sit somewhere you can both relax. That’s all the setup you need.

## Your first practice together

Choose a short practice and press play. The most powerful thing you can do is take the breaths alongside your child — when they see you practicing too, it stops being homework and becomes a shared moment.

## Make it a habit

Like at school, consistency matters more than length. A couple of minutes at the same time each day will do more than a long session now and then. Follow your child’s lead, keep it light, and let it be something you both look forward to.
