Connecting a roster means accounts are created automatically and stay current as staff and students change — and everyone can sign in with one click. This page covers your options and how to set each one up.

## Rostering options

Inner Explorer connects with the providers most districts already use. Pick the one your district has in place — you generally won’t need more than one.

## ClassLink

Add Inner Explorer from the ClassLink application library, then approve the roster share from your ClassLink Management Console. Once approved, your schools, staff, and rosters flow in automatically and staff can launch straight from their LaunchPad.

## Clever

Search for Inner Explorer in your Clever dashboard and share the schools you want to include. After you authorize the connection, accounts are provisioned automatically and users sign in through the Clever Portal.

## Google

Enable Google single sign-on so staff can use their school Workspace accounts. Google handles authentication only — you’ll still add schools and assign roles, or pair Google with one of the rostering options above for automatic provisioning.

## How syncing works

Once connected, your roster refreshes on a regular schedule. New staff and students appear automatically, and people who leave lose access without any manual cleanup. You can trigger a manual sync at any time if you’ve just made changes on the provider side.
