Most districts have nothing to set up. What you do depends on how your staff sign in, and whether you want accounts created before people log in.

## How your staff sign in

<HelpTable label="What each sign-in method requires">

| Sign-in method     | What your district has to do        |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Email and password | Nothing                             |
| Google             | Nothing                             |
| ClassLink          | Install the Inner Explorer app once |
| Clever             | Install the Inner Explorer app once |
| Canvas             | Install the Inner Explorer LTI app  |

</HelpTable>

## Getting your schools and your team into the platform

Send your Inner Explorer partner a CSV listing your schools and the administrator at each one. We set those up for you, and you will find them under the **Admin** tab.

From there, administrators invite their educators using the invite link in the **Admin** tab.

## Installing the app: ClassLink and Clever

A district administrator installs the Inner Explorer app once from your ClassLink or Clever dashboard. It applies across the whole district, and only a district administrator can do it.

## Rostering is optional

Turning on rosters lets us create accounts before anyone signs in, so staff can sign in without waiting for an invitation. It is available with ClassLink and Clever, and Clever requires a Clever license.

Without rostering, single sign-on still works. The first time someone signs in, they are added as a teacher.

## Who invites whom

Inner Explorer invites your district administrators. District administrators invite school administrators. District and school administrators both invite teachers.

District administrators can also generate a join link for teachers to use instead of an invitation.
