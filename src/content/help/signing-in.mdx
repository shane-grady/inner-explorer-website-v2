Inner Explorer supports a few ways to sign in, depending on how your school set up access. This page covers all of them, then walks through your first sign-in and what to do if something goes wrong.

## Ways to sign in

Most schools use a single sign-on provider so you can get in with the account you already use every day. If you’re not sure which applies to you, ask your school administrator.

<CardGrid columns={2}>
  <Card title="Email & password">

  <Card title="Google">

  </Card>
  <Card title="ClassLink">

## Signing in for the first time

If you received an email invitation to join your school, follow these steps to set up your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation email">
    Look for a message from Inner Explorer and select **Accept invitation**. Check your spam folder
    if you don’t see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your password">
    Choose a password of at least eight characters. If your school uses Google, ClassLink, or
    Clever, you can skip this and use that button instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your name and role">
    Check that your name and your role (educator, administrator, or family) are correct, then
    continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press play">
    You’ll land on your home screen, ready to choose a classroom and start your first practice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="good">
  Didn’t get an invitation? Your school may provision accounts automatically through a roster sync.
  In that case, just use the Google, ClassLink, or Clever button — no invitation needed.
</Callout>

## Single sign-on

Single sign-on (SSO) lets you reach Inner Explorer with the same account you use for the rest of your school tools. There’s no extra password to remember, and access is added or removed automatically as your roster changes.

On the sign-in screen, choose the button that matches your school — Google, ClassLink, or Clever — and you’ll be brought straight to your home screen. Administrators can read how to enable SSO in [Connect rostering &amp; single sign-on](/help/rostering-sso).
