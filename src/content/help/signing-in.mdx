Inner Explorer supports a few ways to sign in, depending on how your school set up access. This page covers all of them, then walks through your first sign-in and what to do if something goes wrong.

## Ways to sign in

Most schools use a single sign-on provider so you can get in with the account you already use every day. If you’re not sure which applies to you, ask your school administrator.

## Setting up single sign-on

Administrators set this up once for the whole school. Single sign-on lets your staff reach Inner Explorer with the accounts they already use for the rest of your school tools, so there is no extra password for anyone to remember, and access is added or removed automatically as your roster changes.

If you don’t see your school’s button on the sign-in screen, single sign-on hasn’t been connected yet. Administrators can turn it on in [Connect rostering &amp; single sign-on](/rostering-sso/). Educators and families should ask their school administrator to enable it.
