Inner Explorer supports a few ways to sign in, depending on how your school set up access. This page covers all of them, then walks through your first sign-in.

Brand new user? Skip to [signing in for the first time](#new-users-signing-in-for-the-first-time).

## Ways to sign in

**If you are an existing user of Inner Explorer, welcome back!** You can use your existing email and password to log in below. **We recommend using the email and password method to get started this year.**

Many schools use a single sign-on provider so you can get in with the account you already use every day. If your single sign-on is not working, or if Inner Explorer does not appear in your available apps, your administrator may not have connected it yet.

  <Card title="Email & password">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Google">
    Go to [app.innerexplorer.com](http://app.innerexplorer.com). Sign in with your school Google
    Workspace account in one click, with no separate password.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ClassLink">
    There are two ways to sign in with ClassLink:

    1. Go to [app.innerexplorer.com](http://app.innerexplorer.com) and choose **Continue with
       ClassLink**. Sign in to ClassLink and follow the steps to set up your profile.
    2. If available, log in to ClassLink with your school’s single sign-on. Launch Inner Explorer
       from your ClassLink LaunchPad and you’re signed in automatically. Follow the steps to set up
       your profile.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Clever">
    There are two ways to sign in with Clever:

    1. Go to [app.innerexplorer.com](http://app.innerexplorer.com) and choose **Continue with
       Clever**. Sign in to Clever and follow the steps to set up your profile.
    2. If available, log in to your Clever Portal with your school’s single sign-on. Add the app to
       your app tiles, select the Inner Explorer tile, and follow the steps to set up your profile.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Canvas">
    Open Inner Explorer from your Courses in Canvas and you are signed in automatically. If you do
    not see it there, your district may have placed it elsewhere or not installed it yet.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Callout type="note">
  The first time you sign in with Google, ClassLink, or Clever, you will be asked to select your
  district and school. That is a one-time step.
</Callout>

## Setting up single sign-on

Administrators set this up once for the whole school. Single sign-on lets your staff reach Inner Explorer with the accounts they already use for the rest of your school tools, so there is no extra password for anyone to remember, and access is added or removed automatically as your roster changes.

If Inner Explorer does not appear in your Clever, ClassLink, or Canvas account, it has not been connected yet. Administrators can turn it on in [Connect rostering and single sign-on](/rostering-sso/). Educators and families should ask their school administrator to enable it.
