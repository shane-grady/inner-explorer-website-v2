A classroom is your home base in Inner Explorer. It keeps each group’s progress, series, and journal entries together. You can create as many classrooms as you need (one per class period, grade level, or group) and switch between them in a couple of clicks. Setting one up takes about a minute.

## Add a classroom

Your classrooms live in the classroom menu, in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. If your school connects a roster, your classes may already be listed. In that case you can skip ahead and simply select yours.

## Switch between classrooms

Once you have more than one classroom, moving between them works the same way: open the classroom menu in the top-right corner and select the classroom you want. A checkmark shows which one is active, and the switch happens instantly: no signing out, no lost progress.

<Callout type="tip">
  Each classroom keeps its own day-by-day progress. If your morning class is on Day 12 and your
  afternoon class is on Day 3, switching classrooms always drops you exactly where that group left
  off.
</Callout>

## Choosing the right experience

The experience you pick helps Inner Explorer recommend practices that fit your students’ age and attention span. You can change it, along with the language, anytime from the classroom’s settings.

## Add students

Adding students is optional. The daily practice works as a whole-class experience whether or not individual students are listed. If you’d like students to keep their own journals, you can add them by name or let your roster sync populate the class automatically.

<Callout type="note">
  You don’t need a single student listed to begin. Many teachers run the practice as a whole-class
  routine and add students later if they want individual journaling.
</Callout>
