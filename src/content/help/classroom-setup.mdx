A classroom is your home base in Inner Explorer — it keeps each group’s progress, series, and journal entries together. Setting one up takes about a minute.

## Create a classroom

From your home screen, select **Add a classroom**. If your school connects a roster, your classes may already be listed — in that case you can skip ahead and simply select yours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Add a classroom">
    You’ll find it on your home screen, beneath any classrooms you’ve already created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your class">
    Use a name you’ll recognize at a glance, like “Period 3 — Science” or “Ms. Lee’s Kindergarten.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Your new classroom appears on the home screen, ready to use.</Step>
</Steps>

## Add your class details

Choosing a grade band helps Inner Explorer recommend practices that fit your students’ age and attention span. You can change it anytime from the classroom’s settings.

## Add students

Adding students is optional. The daily practice works as a whole-class experience whether or not individual students are listed. If you’d like students to keep their own journals, you can add them by name or let your roster sync populate the class automatically.

<Callout type="tip">
  You don’t need a single student listed to begin. Many teachers run the practice as a whole-class
  routine and add students later if they want individual journaling.
</Callout>

## You’re ready

That’s it — your classroom is set up. Next, learn how to choose a practice and lead your first session with confidence in [Lead your first practice](/help/first-practice).
