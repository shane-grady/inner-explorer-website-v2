## Introduction

At Inner Explorer Inc (“the company,” “we,” or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to
protecting it as described in this privacy policy (“Policy” or the “Privacy Policy”). This Policy
describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you use our
product, Inner Explorer (“the product,” “the service,” “the website,” “the app,” or “product”), and
our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information. Your
use of the Product and Services and any information you provide on the Services are subject to the
terms of this Privacy Policy, which is incorporated into and subject to our Terms of Service. Where
we have signed a student data privacy agreement (“DPA”) or similar agreement with a school,
district, or educational agency, the terms of that agreement control in the event of any conflict
with this Policy.

<Callout type="tip" label="We do not collect student information">
  Inner Explorer is an educator-led, whole-classroom program. Educators play audio-guided
  mindfulness practices for the classroom from their own accounts. Students never create accounts,
  never log in, and never enter, upload, or generate any information in the product. We do not
  collect, store, or process any student personal information, student education records, or student
  metadata of any kind. The only personal information we collect belongs to the adult educators and
  staff who administer the program.
</Callout>

## Data collection

We collect Personal Information you provide to us (such as the information you enter when creating
an educator account), as well as Personal Information collected automatically (such as IP address
and product usage data) as described below. For the purpose of this Policy, Personal Information is
defined as information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with or describes
an individual or household, either directly or indirectly.

Limited Personal Information is processed on our behalf by the service providers that operate our
platform and analytics, as described in the “Platform, hosting, and service providers” section
below. These service providers act only on our instructions, under written agreements, and are
prohibited from using your information for their own purposes. We do not combine data with
additional data from third-party sources, and no third party is permitted to collect Personal
Information through our product for its own use.

Collection is limited to what the product requires. Users cannot create or upload content. User
information does not have to be shared or revealed in order to participate in social interactions.
Aggregated or combined information, representing a collective summary of data, is treated with the
same level of confidentiality and protection as personally identifiable information (PII) on our
platform.

## How we use your personal information

Collection and use of data is limited to product requirements, and the use of Personal Information
is limited to the purposes for which it was collected. This includes:

- Service delivery: providing, operating, securing, and improving the service.
- Analytics and research using aggregated or de-identified information, for example demonstrating
  program usage and effectiveness to the schools and districts we serve.
- Communications with account holders about the service, including new education product updates,
  features, or services.
- Compliance with legal obligations.
- Retention as described below.

We do not use Personal Information for targeted advertising. We do not sell Personal Information. We
do not permit partners or third parties to create data profiles for personalized advertisements, and
data is not collected by the product for the purpose of tracking users across other sites or
services.

## Platform, hosting, and service providers

The Inner Explorer platform is built and hosted on infrastructure provided by Spotlight Series
Holdings, Inc. (d/b/a Blueprint), our platform services provider. All Inner Explorer data is hosted
on MongoDB Atlas on Google Cloud Platform within the United States (us-central1 region). The
platform employs the following safeguards:

- Encryption in transit using SSL/TLS (TLS 1.2 or higher) and encryption at rest using AES-256
  standards;
- Secure Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) configurations and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), so only
  authorized personnel can access relevant data;
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and strong identity management controls protecting
  administrative access;
- Continuous audit logging of security events, administrative activities, and system access;
- Automated backups, disaster recovery procedures, and redundant cloud infrastructure; and
- A security architecture incorporating controls aligned with NIST SP 800-53, including identity and
  access management, encryption, audit logging, continuous monitoring, incident response, and risk
  management.

We also use Amplitude, Inc. as our analytics provider to understand how educators use the product so
we can improve it. Amplitude processes educator account usage data only, acts solely as a service
provider on our instructions, and maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001/27017/27018 certifications
with encryption of data in transit and at rest. No student information exists in our environment, so
no student information is hosted by Blueprint or transmitted to Amplitude.

Both providers are bound by written agreements that require security protections no less stringent
than our own commitments to the schools and districts we serve, prohibit any sale of data or use of
data for advertising, and require secure deletion of data. A current list of our service providers
is available upon request at info@innerexplorer.com.

## Data retention and deletion

We maintain a formal Data Retention Policy to establish clear guidelines on the lifecycle management
of our data, ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, bolster data security, and
demonstrate a commitment to responsible and transparent data management practices within our
organization. Personal Data that we collect will be deleted when it is no longer necessary to
provide you with our services. We delete Personal Data upon account cancellation or termination, and
upon expiration or termination of a school or district agreement we securely delete the associated
data from active systems, backups, and logs using industry-standard secure deletion methods; written
certification of deletion is available to educational agencies upon request.

In some cases, we may retain Personal Data beyond the length of time that you have an account with
us in order to resolve disputes, comply with legal and compliance obligations, or otherwise.
Anonymized data, which is no longer attached to you personally, may be retained for longer periods
of time.

## How we share your personal information

Collected information is only shared with the service providers described above, or with third
parties the customer has authorized through a contractual arrangement. Contractual limits are placed
on all third-party data use. Personal information is not shared for third-party marketing. Personal
information is not sold or rented to third parties. Where user information is shared for reporting
purposes, it is shared in an anonymous, aggregated, or de-identified format.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, user
information may be transferred only to a successor that assumes, in writing, the privacy and
security obligations of this Policy and of our agreements with educational agencies, and we will
provide written notice to affected schools and districts as required by those agreements and
applicable law.

We do not display your personal information that you have submitted to us or that we have collected
in the product. Third parties with access to information are required to provide the same security
protections as the company.

## Student data and education records

Because students never use accounts or enter information, Inner Explorer does not collect student
education records or student personal information as defined under the Family Educational Rights and
Privacy Act (FERPA), the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), or state student privacy
laws (including, for example, New York Education Law §2-d, Illinois SOPPA, and California SOPIPA).
Where required, we enter into student data privacy agreements with schools, districts, and
educational agencies and, in performing services under those agreements, we act as a “school
official” under the direction and control of the educational agency. If our data collection
practices were ever to change such that student information would be collected, we would notify our
school and district partners immediately and in advance, and such collection would be governed by
our agreements with those agencies.

We will never sell student information, use it for targeted advertising, or build advertising
profiles. There is no student information in our systems to begin with, and our agreements with
educational agencies prohibit these practices in all cases.

personalized advertising.

process, we will collect certain information to establish and manage your account effectively. This
information may include, but is not limited to, your name, school-issued email address, and a
password of your choice. Your account allows you to personalize your experience, track your
activities, and access additional features. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security
of your account information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. Our platform is equipped with
access control mechanisms, allowing you to define and manage permissions for your data.

the technologies necessary to operate the service: session and authentication technologies that keep
you signed in securely, and analytics identifiers used by our analytics provider to understand
product usage by educator accounts. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies or

## Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect data throughout its lifecycle. All data in
transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher, and all data at rest is stored in an encrypted format
using AES-256 standards. Employee and physical access to user information is limited through
role-based access controls and least-privilege principles, administrative access is protected by
multi-factor authentication, and security events and administrative activities are continuously
logged to support monitoring and forensic investigation. Our security program is aligned with
nationally recognized frameworks, including the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.

We maintain a written incident response plan consistent with industry standards and applicable
federal and state law. Users are provided notice in the event of a data breach affecting their
personal information, and we notify affected school and district partners within the timelines
required by our agreements with them (in most cases within 72 hours of confirmation of an incident)
and by applicable law.

## Your privacy rights

Except as otherwise provided, this section applies to residents of states where they have privacy
laws applicable to us that grant their residents the rights described below. For purposes of this
section, “personal information” has the meaning given to “personal data,” “personal information,” or
similar terms under the applicable privacy laws of the state in which you reside. Please note that
not all rights listed below may be afforded to all users, and that if you are not a resident of the
relevant states, you may not be able to exercise these rights. In addition, we may not be able to
process your request if you do not provide us with sufficient detail to allow us to confirm your
identity or understand and respond to it. In some cases, we may provide a different privacy notice
to certain categories of residents of these states, such as job applicants, in which case that
notice will apply with respect to the activities it describes instead of this section.

**Verification:** Upon receipt of your request, we will initiate the identity verification process to
confirm that you are the individual for whom we hold information in our system. This verification
procedure necessitates your cooperation in supplying relevant information, allowing us to compare it
with the details you previously furnished. Depending on the nature of your request, we may request
specific information to align with our existing records, or we may reach out to you using a
previously provided communication method (such as phone or email). In certain situations,
alternative verification methods may be employed based on the circumstances. Any personal
information disclosed in your request will be used solely to confirm your identity or validate your
authority to make the request. Any supplementary information you provide for verification will be
promptly deleted upon the completion of the verification process.

**Authorized agents:** Your authorized agent may be able to make a request on your behalf. However,
we may need to verify your authorized agent’s identity and authority to act on your behalf. We may
require a copy of a valid power of attorney given to your authorized agent pursuant to applicable
law. If you have not provided your agent with such a power of attorney, we may ask you to take
additional steps permitted by law to verify that your request is authorized.

- **Access:** You can request a copy of the personal information that we have collected about you.
- **Correction:** You can request that we correct inaccurate personal information that we have
  collected about you.
- **Deletion:** You can request that we delete your personal information. Please note that once we
  process a deletion request, we may be unable to provide access or support to any past products or
  services.

We never sell any data to third parties, and we do not “share” personal information with advertising
partners for targeted advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA and similar state laws,
so there is no sale or sharing to opt out of. You have the right to request details on the personal
information that we disclosed to a third party for a business purpose.

## Children’s privacy notice

Our service is used in classrooms serving preschool through high school students, including children
from 2 to 18 years old. The product is operated entirely by adult educators: students do not create
accounts, do not log in, and cannot interact with the product or with other users. We do not collect
Personal Information from any children, including those 18 years old and younger, and no student
information of any kind, including names, identifiers, usage data, or metadata, is collected,
stored, or processed by us or by our service providers. Because we collect no information from
children, the parental consent requirements of COPPA are not triggered by our product. This service
also provides guidance for parents or guardians to ensure a safe experience for their children, and
we will notify our school and district partners immediately and in advance if these practices were
ever to change.
