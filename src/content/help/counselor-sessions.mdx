Every student practices the Daily Practice with their class, and that stays the foundation. The Counselor Series is built for the settings you work in instead: small groups and one on one.

## Where it fits

The Daily Practice is universal. Every student in the building is learning the same skill at the same time, which means the students you see have almost always practiced it before they get to you.

The Counselor Series picks that up for Tier 2 and Tier 3 support. Same skill, sized for a small circle or a single student across a desk.

## Start your sessions with a practice

Open with a Counselor Series practice before the conversation begins. A few minutes of settling helps a student arrive, and because it’s the same practice they know from class, there’s nothing new to teach.

Students often show up already dysregulated. Reflection is hard to reach in that state, so the practice comes first and the talking comes after.

## Choosing a practice

These don’t need to be played in order. Pick the one that fits the student in front of you, or work through the series and settle on the ones you come back to.

<Callout type="tip">
  You don’t have to lead the practice. The audio does that, which frees you to be present in the
  room with the student rather than running the session.
</Callout>
