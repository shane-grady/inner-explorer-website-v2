Inner Explorer is intentionally simple. Most of what you need is one or two clicks away. Here’s a quick tour of the places you’ll spend your time.

## Your home screen

When you sign in, you land on your home screen. Educators see their classrooms; administrators see their district dashboard. From here you can pick a classroom, jump back into a series you’ve started, or open today’s recommended practice.

## Switching classrooms

If you teach more than one class, each appears as its own card on the home screen. Selecting a classroom keeps its progress, journal entries, and series separate, so every group moves at its own pace.

<Callout type="note">
  You can switch classrooms at any time from the home screen; your place in each series is saved
  automatically.
</Callout>

## The practice player

When you open a practice, the player fills the screen with a calm, themed background. The controls are deliberately minimal: play and pause, skip forward, and a progress bar. Press play and let the guide lead. There’s nothing else to manage.

## Lessons & library

Beyond the Daily Practice, the library holds full themed series, standalone lessons, and journaling prompts. This is where you go to find something for a specific moment: a grounding breath before a test, or a kindness practice after a hard day.

### The series available to you

**Daily Practice** is the core of the program: 180 practices, one for every day of the school year. It runs in sequence, and it’s the one series you’ll want to follow straight through from Day 1. See [Launch your first practice](/first-practice/) to get started.

The rest are there to draw on alongside it:

- **Transitions Series** to reset a class between activities: coming back from recess, moving from one subject to the next, or closing out the day.
- **School Safety Series** for after a drill or a lockdown, when a class needs to come down from high alert before returning to the normal school day.
- **Counselor Series** for small group and one-on-one settings. See [Using the Counselor Series](/counselor-sessions/).
- **Educator Well-Being Series** for educators to practice on their own, in staff meetings, or in hard moments.
- **Sound practices** for settling with sound alone, when the room needs a quiet moment without a spoken guide.

Every plan includes all of these, so they’re already in your library.

Play these in order if you can. They’re built to build on each other, and going straight through gives you the whole arc. That said, plenty of educators land on a few favorites and return to those whenever the moment calls for it, which works just as well.

<Callout type="note">
  **STEM + M** blends daily mindfulness with STEM lessons, delivered in a project-based learning
  (PBL) format aligned to the Next Generation Science Standards. It’s an add-on rather than part of
  every plan, so you’ll only see it in your library if your school has added it.
</Callout>
