Inner Explorer brings a few quiet minutes of guided mindfulness into the school day. This page explains what the platform does and how to begin. No prior experience with mindfulness required.

## What Inner Explorer is

Inner Explorer is a daily, audio-guided mindfulness program built for K–12 classrooms. Each session is short, usually three to ten minutes, and led by a recorded guide, so the adult in the room can settle in and practice alongside students rather than perform.

Practices are organized into themed series that build gently week over week, moving from simple breath awareness toward attention, kindness, and emotional regulation.

<Callout type="tip">
  You don’t have to lead the practice; the audio does. Your role is simply to press play and take
  the breaths with your students.
</Callout>
