Inner Explorer brings 5-10 minutes of guided mindfulness into the school day. This page covers what
the program is, how a daily practice works, and where to start. You do not need any experience with
mindfulness to begin.

## What Inner Explorer is

Inner Explorer is a daily, audio guided mindfulness program built for PreK through 12 school
communities. A recorded guide leads every practice, so the adult in the room can practice alongside
students.

Practices are grouped into series that build across the school year.

### Quick facts

- 5 to 10 minutes a day
- 180 practices, one for every day of the school year
- Audio guided and screen free, apart from a short intro video
- Available in English and Spanish
- No lesson planning, prep, or training required
- Built for PreK through 12, with practices matched to each grade range

## Why schools practice every day

Stress shows up in the body before it shows up in behavior. When a student’s nervous system is on
high alert, focus, memory, and self control are harder to reach, no matter how good the lesson is.

A daily practice gives the nervous system a chance to settle. Over time, students get better at
noticing what they feel and choosing what to do next. The adults in the room build the same skill,
because everyone practices together.

That is why the practice is short and why it happens every day. Consistency is what makes the skill
stick.

  the breaths along with everyone else.
</Callout>

Most students close their eyes. Anyone who would rather not can follow a still image on screen or
bow their heads.
