As an administrator, you set up the structure that everyone else works within: your district or school, the schools beneath it, and the educators and administrators on your team. This page walks through that initial setup.

## Accept your invitation

Your Inner Explorer partner sends an invitation to the email you provided. Select **Accept invitation**, create your password or sign in with single sign-on, and you’ll land on your district dashboard.

## Set up your profile

Confirm your district or organization name and time zone. These details shape how your reports are labeled and how engagement dates are calculated, so it’s worth getting them right at the start.

## Add schools

Add each school in your district so adoption and impact can be tracked per site. If you connect a roster, schools are created for you automatically; see [Connect rostering &amp; single sign-on](/rostering-sso/).

## Invite your team

Invite educators and fellow administrators by email, or let them sign in through single sign-on once your roster is connected. Assign each person a role so they see the right experience: educators get classrooms, administrators get the dashboard.

<Callout type="tip">
  For larger rollouts, connect a roster instead of inviting people one by one. Accounts are then
  created and kept up to date automatically as staff join or leave.
</Callout>
