As an administrator, you set up the structure that everyone else works within: your district or school, the schools beneath it, and the educators and administrators on your team. This page walks through that initial setup.

## Accept your invitation

Your Inner Explorer partner sends an invitation to the email you provided. Select **Accept invitation**, create your password or sign in with single sign-on, and you’ll land on your district dashboard.

## Set up your profile

Confirm your district or organization name and time zone. These details shape how your reports are labeled and how engagement dates are calculated, so it’s worth getting them right at the start.

## Check your schools

Your schools are populated for you. Open the **Admin** tab and confirm that every school in your district is listed, so adoption can be tracked per site.

If anything is missing, share an accurate roster of your schools and their administrators with your Inner Explorer partner.

## Invite your team

Invite educators and fellow administrators by email with the custom generated link under the **Admin** tab, or let them sign in through single sign-on once your roster is connected. Assign each person a role so they see the right experience: educators get classrooms, school administrators get the dashboard.
