As an administrator, your role is to monitor how practice is taking hold across your schools. You do not have to build out your district: we set up your account and your schools for you before you ever sign in. This page walks through confirming that everything came through correctly, then inviting the educators and administrators who are new to Inner Explorer, which is the one part that is yours.

## Accept your invitation

Your Inner Explorer partner sends an invitation to the email you provided. Select **Accept invitation**, create your password or sign in with single sign-on, and you’ll land on your district dashboard.

## Set up your profile

Confirm your district or organization name and time zone. These details shape how your reports are labeled and how engagement dates are calculated, so it’s worth getting them right at the start.

## Check your schools

Your schools are populated for you. Open the **Admin** tab and confirm that every school in your district is listed, so adoption can be tracked per site.

If a school is missing, or anything is listed incorrectly, reach out to your Inner Explorer partner and we’ll correct it. There is nothing for you to add or edit here.

## Invite new educators and administrators

This is the step that is yours, and it is the one that gets Inner Explorer into classrooms.

Invite any educator or school administrator who is new to Inner Explorer by email with the custom generated link under the **Admin** tab, or let them sign in through single sign-on once your roster is connected. Assign each person a role so they see the right experience: educators get classrooms, school administrators get the dashboard. You can also add new school admin and educators individually under the **Admin** tab following the instructional video below.
