# FINAL V board inventory

Every repeating pattern across the 39 boards of the **FINAL V** page of the canvas
"Inner Explorer — Website" (https://claude.ai/artifact/6gx8tapeaDzTeSSMD7R31s), read board by
board on 2026-10-10, and where each one lives in code. Status: **foundation** (built in the
design-foundation PR), **page-local** (built by the page PR that needs it, from the shared
parts; it joins `src/components/blocks/` when a second page needs it), **deferred** (not
settled on FINAL V). Page PRs update this file as they add patterns.

Boards are named by their page: Home (Main, Home-Desktop-2, Home-Mobile, Home-Mobile-2,
Home-Mobile-Scrolled), About (4), Article (5), Case Studies (2), Contact (3), Newsroom (2),
Platform (5), Pricing (2), Research (5), Why (6).
